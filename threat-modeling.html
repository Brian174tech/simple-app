GUIDES / THREAT MODELING
MAP THE TERRAIN

Ask better
what-ifs.

Threat modeling is a structured conversation about what you are building, what could go wrong, and what you will do about it. Repeat it whenever the system changes.

STEP 01

Define the system

Draw the boundaries. Identify components, users, services, data stores, and the flows between them.

ASK // What are we building?
STEP 02

Find what matters

List sensitive data and critical operations. Note where trust changes as information moves through the system.

ASK // What needs protection?
STEP 03

Imagine failure

Consider who might misuse the system, what they could reach, and how mistakes or outages could affect people.

ASK // What could go wrong?
STEP 04

Choose defenses

Rank risks by likelihood and impact. Assign practical mitigations, owners, and a point to review each decision.

ASK // What will we do?
!
KEEP IT USEFUL

A threat model is a living tool.

Keep it lightweight and specific to your system. Update it when data flows, integrations, user roles, or assumptions change. Record accepted risks and who owns them.
