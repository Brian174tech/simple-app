PRE-FLIGHT REVIEW

Ship with
confidence.

Use these prompts to guide a team review before release. Not every item fits every system; document the reasoning and the remaining risks.

◈

Access & identity

01—04
  • Every user and service has a distinct identity.
  • Access follows least privilege and is reviewed regularly.
  • Authorization is checked on every sensitive operation.
  • Secrets are not stored in source code or exposed in logs.
⌁

Data & input

05—08
  • Only necessary data is collected, retained, and shared.
  • Inputs are validated at system boundaries.
  • Sensitive data is protected in transit and at rest where appropriate.
  • Errors do not reveal secrets or unnecessary internal details.
⌕

Operations & response

09—12
  • Dependencies are known and have a maintenance plan.
  • Security-relevant events can be investigated without logging sensitive values.
  • Backups and recovery procedures have been tested.
  • There is a clear owner and response path for reported issues.
